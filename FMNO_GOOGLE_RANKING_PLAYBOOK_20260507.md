## The professional ranking model

1. Technical SEO first
- 200 status pages
- robots.txt allows crawl
- sitemap includes canonical URLs
- unique titles/descriptions
- exact-match brand signals
- schema: Organization, WebSite, Article, FAQ, Breadcrumb
- internal links from homepage and hub pages

2. Search intent beats keyword stuffing
Google is rewarding pages that solve the searcher’s real problem, not mechanical keyword pages. For FMNO this means pages that answer:
- What is Fix My Name Online?
- How do I check my online reputation?
- Can old Google results be removed?
- What do I do about fake Google reviews?
- Who provides online reputation management in Australia?

3. Topical authority cluster
A homepage alone is not enough. Build a cluster around the service:
- Brand page
- Learn hub
- Service pages
- Problem guides
- FAQ/schema pages
- Internal links between all of them

4. Competitor gap / SaaS playbook
The SaaS SEO pattern is to avoid only chasing the hardest head keyword. Build supporting pages around lower-competition, high-intent searches first, then link them back into the commercial pages.

5. Helpful content and E-E-A-T
Content must be specific, careful, and credible. For reputation repair, this means honest limits and no fake guarantees. Trust language matters because reputation repair can look spammy if overclaimed.

6. Authority and discovery
Google still needs external signals. The site should be linked from owned profiles and verified properties:
- Google Search Console
- Facebook Page
- Instagram bio
- First Page Strategy / MadisonJade safe mentions if brand architecture allows
- Substack/Tumblr/company profiles
