Questions

What happens after I pay?

You go straight to your private workspace. Enter your facts, and your request letter, follow-up and second letter are created for that link. You can edit them, then send them yourself.

Can I use it on another device?

Yes. Your letters are saved online. Use the link from your payment confirmation, or get a new access link by email from the recovery page.

What if they say no?

Record the answer in your workspace. The free plan for your problem lists what to try next in your country.

Can I get a refund?

Yes, within 7 days if you haven’t created your letters yet, straight from your workspace. Your Australian Consumer Law rights always apply. See refunds.
