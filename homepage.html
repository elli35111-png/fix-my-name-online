GOOD QUESTIONS

No guessing.
Here's the detail.

What does the free Snapshot actually do?

It collects your location, concern and permission, then suggests a next-step route from your answers. It is not a live Google search, verified reputation score or human review.

Can I handle it myself without paying?

Yes. The free self-service desk provides a checklist, official pathway links, editable notes and progress tracking. Export your notes or choose to save them in this browser.

Will FMNO contact anyone for me?

No. You verify the facts, choose the route and submit any external request yourself. Publishers, platforms and search engines decide outcomes.

Do I need the link already?

No link is required for the free intake. Explain the result or exact search phrase instead. The paid one-URL workspace needs a specific public URL.

Where are my self-service notes stored?

They stay in the open page by default. You can choose to save on this browser or download an export. Browser storage is not encrypted or synced to an account; avoid it on shared devices. The desk does not upload your notes to FMNO.
